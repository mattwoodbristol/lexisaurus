# 吗 — Yes/no questions

**Pattern:** statement + 吗？

## Grammar

Turn any statement into a yes/no question by adding 吗 at the end. The word order does not change.

Answer by repeating the verb or adjective: 你忙吗？ — 忙。/ 不忙。

### Examples

- 你喝咖啡吗？ — Do you drink coffee?
- 他忙吗？ — Is he busy?

### Key points

- Word order stays the same as the statement
- Answer with the verb, not with "yes": 喝 / 不喝

## Drills

1. Do you like tea? → 你喜欢茶吗？
2. Is it cold today? → 今天冷吗？
3. Are you busy? → 你忙吗？

## Common errors

- ✗ 你是喜欢茶吗？ → ✓ 你喜欢茶吗？ — no 是 before a verb

## Grammar points

- **吗 questions**: statement + 吗 makes a yes/no question

## Conversation

Ask the tutor yes/no questions with 吗 about their day.
